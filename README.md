# Hiro.fm for Claude

Your own AI for your Hiro show. [Hiro](https://hiro.fm) sells private podcasts and audio courses for coaches, course creators and authors. This plugin adds the `/hiro` skill to Claude: 31 ready-made playbooks that work from your show's Show Soul and the live numbers Hiro already has, so Claude never guesses at your downloads, sales or completion.

## What you need

A Hiro account, connected to Claude. Open https://app.hiro.fm/ai/mcp in Hiro and follow the three steps: copy the URL, add it as a connector, sign in. Installing this plugin also offers the Hiro.fm connector. No Hiro credits are used by the connector or by this skill.

## How to use it

In any Claude chat, type `/hiro` and say what you want, for example `/hiro set up my show soul`. Type `/hiro` on its own to see the playbooks grouped and pick one.

## The 31 playbooks

**Set up:** set up my show soul; learn my voice from my episodes; find the best moment in each episode.

**Create content:** this week's email about my newest episode; a 5-email welcome series; an episode into 10 Instagram captions and a LinkedIn post; clip captions for every best moment; my next 6 episodes from what listeners download most; 5 better titles and descriptions; this month's content plan; review an episode and tell me what to improve.

**Reuse content:** each episode into a carousel or quote graphic.

**Hiro tools:** more leads or booked calls; a free series or lead magnet; a book, PDF or doc into episodes; a newsletter from my episodes; find listeners for my show; new cover art; nudge listeners who haven't played in 30 days.

**Find listeners:** a pitch to get me on a podcast as a guest; what ChatGPT and Perplexity recommend for my topic, and where I show up.

**Sell:** a plan to hit a listener number by a date; what my next paid offer should be.

**Improve:** the one thing holding my show back; which episode loses the most people; how my sales page compares to the top shows in my niche; which free sample turns into the most sales; change my show description to an option; how last week's changes did.

**Routine:** a Monday report on my show; this month's numbers against last month, by source.

## What Claude keeps in Hiro

Each show has a Show Soul: identity, voice, best moments, tracked links and a history of what the AI did and changed. The playbooks read it first and write back what they learn, so the next session picks up where the last one ended. Nothing is changed in your business without asking you first.
